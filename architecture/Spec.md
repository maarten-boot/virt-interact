# Spec: virt-interact

Status: draft. Waiting for the scenario description.

Items are numbered `SPEC-nnn` so that Plan.md and TestCases.md can refer to them.

## Standing requirements

### SPEC-001: UI colour and contrast
Any GUI or TUI component:
- defaults to a white background with black text;
- keeps every foreground/background pair at a WCAG contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1;
- lets the user raise the minimum contrast interactively and through a config file, with presets AA (4.5:1), AAA (7:1) and Max (21:1, pure black/white), plus a custom minimum ratio.
